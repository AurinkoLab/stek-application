Kart2Grid Educational Program

Application Concept Brief for STEK Call 2027–2031

Budget: €1,000,000 Duration: 2027–2031 (3 Phases) Format: Open Learning Modules Target: VET & Higher Education

The 2035 Challenge

By 2035, Artificial Intelligence will handle much of the calculating and diagnosing in the energy sector. However, energy specialists must still understand physical phenomena and work safely on high-voltage equipment.

The problem: Providing hands-on experience with 800V systems (like EV batteries or grid storage) is dangerous, expensive, and difficult to scale in standard vocational colleges. Kart2Grid makes it scalable and safe.

The Consortium

Our consortium perfectly balances academic rigor, vocational reality, and technological innovation to meet STEK requirements:

  • Lead University (LUT / HY): Program direction, academic validation, and scaling via the Teacher Academy.
  • Varia (VET Partner): Vocational piloting, pedagogical integration, and workplace learning.
  • XR Partner: Developing immersive, open-licensed XR environments.
  • AurinkoLab: Open hardware blueprints and the Attractiveness strand (Challenge).
  • CSC (Subcontractor): 2035+ foresight skills mapping.

The Hybrid Learning Methodology

1. Build Safely (48V)

Students manually build real, scalable energy systems (solar go-karts and boats) operating at a safe 48V. They learn the physical reality of solar PV, batteries, and telemetry.

2. Practice in XR (800V)

High-risk scenarios are rehearsed in Virtual Reality. Students safely practice lockout/tagout, thermal runaway response, and managing EV fast-charging.

3. Verify AI (Data)

An AI Co-pilot predicts energy output and diagnoses faults. Crucially, students must use a physical multimeter to verify the AI's hypothesis on the hardware.
